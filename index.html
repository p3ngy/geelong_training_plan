Base, weeks 1 to 626 Oct to 6 DecRebuild running slowly to about 18 km a week, steady aerobic riding with one quality session, squad swims, two gym sessions. Tests in week 4. Race 1 (sprint) on 6 Dec.
Build, weeks 7 to 127 Dec to 17 JanBrick runs begin, sweet spot work grows, long ride reaches 3:30, long run 12 km. Hiking trip resets week 8. Race 2 (Olympic) on 10 Jan.
Specific, weeks 13 to 1818 Jan to 28 FebRace-power blocks on the TT bike, long ride up to 4:15, long run up to 18 km, peak week 17. Germany block in weeks 15 to 18: indoor bike, pool and run focus.
Taper, weeks 19 to 211 Mar to 21 MarVolume drops, short sharp efforts stay, last long run in week 19. Nothing new in race week. Race day 21 Mar.

Weekly planner

Drag a session to another day in the same week to reschedule it. On a phone, drag from the dots on its right. Tick the circle on a session when you have done it. Delete a session with its × (or the Delete key) and tap Undo if it was a mistake. Keyboard: focus a session, then Alt with the arrow keys. Changes save in this browser; races and travel days are fixed.

light
